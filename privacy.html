Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026.

Local processing

The image, writing, developer, password and calculator tools run in your browser. Files, passwords and text entered into these tools are not sent to a ZentroKit application server. Results are generated on your own device.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-06TDBEDK5W) to understand aggregated site usage. If you decline, the analytics script is not loaded. Your choice is saved in localStorage as zentrokit_analytics_consent. You can reset that choice by clearing this site's local storage in your browser.

Third-party requests

We use Google Fonts to display our typography. Font requests and essential hosting traffic may be processed by third parties under their own policies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access logs.

Advertising

We may add advertising in future. Any future ad integration and consent controls will be updated here before activation. A publisher verification file may be hosted on this domain; it does not by itself mean ads are actively displayed.

Data security

We cannot guarantee security of your device or browser. Avoid using shared or untrusted devices for sensitive data, and do not treat Base64 encoding as encryption.

Contact

For questions, please contact us through the project feedback page.
