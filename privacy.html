Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

Your videos stay on your device

Cutlance Studio operates its editor inside your browser. When you select a video, its contents are decoded, previewed and processed locally with standard browser APIs. Your selected videos, overlay text and exported media are not transmitted to a Cutlance application server. File previews use local object URLs.

Export and browser requirements

The video export feature uses MediaRecorder and Canvas APIs provided by your browser. Exported media is stored temporarily inside your browser until you download or leave the page. Browser support for MP4 varies and WebM may be used as an alternative.

Website hosting and technical access

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process basic technical access logs. Google Fonts may receive technical request information when loaded.

Analytics and advertising

This first version does not enable Google Analytics or targeted advertising scripts. A pre-existing ads.txt publisher declaration may remain at the domain root; that file does not itself load ads or track visitors. If advertising or optional analytics are added later, this policy and applicable consent controls should be updated first.

Storage and cookies

The new editor does not require an account. It does not intentionally store video projects or upload files to remote storage. Your browser or hosting services may have their own operational storage and logs.

Contact

For feedback or privacy queries, contact us via the public support and issue tracker.
