Common questions

Can I edit videos without uploading them?

Yes. This first version processes local files inside a compatible browser. Files are not uploaded to our server.

Can I export an MP4 video?

When your browser supports MP4 recording, you can export MP4. On browsers without MP4 MediaRecorder support, we provide a WebM video download instead. The exact available format is shown inside the editor.

How long does export take?

Our first version records the edited segment in real time. A 20-second section at normal speed takes about 20 seconds to export. Performance depends on your browser and device.
