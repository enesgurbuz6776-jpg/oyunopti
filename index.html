Questions? Here’s what to know.

Can I make videos for TikTok, Reels or Shorts?

Yes. Choose a 9:16 canvas to prepare vertical video. You can also choose 1:1 or 16:9.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Video and audio files are processed locally inside the browser. We do not operate a video-upload service for this editor.

Can I add automatic AI subtitles?

Not yet. This version supports manually timed captions. We prefer to show a working feature rather than advertise an unfinished AI tool.

Why does export take time?

The current browser-based engine records the selected trim range in real time. Large or long clips can take longer. Keep the tab visible while exporting.
